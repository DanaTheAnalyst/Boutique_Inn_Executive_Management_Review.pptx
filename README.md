## Executive Management Presentation

To extend the dashboard beyond reporting, I translated the analysis into an executive-level PowerPoint presentation focused on business decisions and management actions.

The presentation highlights:

- Annual revenue and occupancy performance
- Seasonal demand patterns
- Scheduled rate vs. realized ADR
- Booking lead-time behavior and last-minute discounting
- Distribution economics and OTA commission impact
- Opportunities to improve revenue quality
- Practical management recommendations

### Key Finding

Average scheduled rate was $432 compared with a realized ADR of $388, resulting in a -10.2% rate variance. The analysis also showed materially lower ADR for same-day bookings, suggesting that last-minute discounting contributes to rate dilution.

### Management Focus

The recommendations focus on protecting ADR, improving channel economics, and managing seasonal demand — with the goal of improving net revenue quality without relying on higher room volume.
